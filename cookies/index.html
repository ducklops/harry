Cookie policy

Last updated 20 November 2026.

harryh.xyz is a personal website run by Ducklops. This page explains what the site stores on your device, what it connects to, and the choices you have. In short: no tracking, no advertising and no third-party cookies.

What we store on your device

The site doesn't set any cookies. It uses your browser's local storage instead, which UK law treats the same way, so we ask before storing anything that isn't essential.

If you choose Essential only, the optional items are kept in memory for your visit and forgotten when you close the tab. Nothing optional is written to your device.

The site's owner also uses an editing page (the console) that stores their GitHub sign-in details on their own device when they ask it to. When the owner play-tests a trivia day, the console also leaves a copy of that day on their own device. Visitors never use it.

Services the site connects to

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, GitHub processes your IP address to deliver pages and may keep it in server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Open-Meteo provides the Liverpool weather. Your browser asks Open-Meteo for the current conditions, so Open-Meteo receives your IP address. It sets no cookies.
  • Fonts are hosted on this site itself. Nothing is loaded from Google or any other font service.

Sharing a Flappy score uses your own device's share menu, and the score image is made on your device. Nothing is uploaded by us.

A Tree-Frog Trivia share or challenge link carries your score, your emoji track and the name you typed inside the link itself. It's only seen by whoever you send it to. Nothing is uploaded by us.

A Trolley Trials challenge link carries your route, score and the name you typed inside the link itself. It's only seen by whoever you send it to. Nothing is uploaded by us.

Changing your mind

You can change your choice at any time. Choosing Essential only deletes the optional items from your device straight away. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings.
