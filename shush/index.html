Shush Snake

A noise meter for the jungle. Keep it quiet and the animals stay safe. Get too loud and the snake comes for them, one at a time. Stay quiet for long enough and they come back… one way or another.

Your microphone stays on this device
  • The page only works out how loud it is, about 60 times a second. The sound itself is thrown away straight away. Nothing is recorded.
  • Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. The page is locked so it can't send any data at all, and it uses no cookies.
  • The mic only turns on when you press the button below, and turns off when you press Mic on, switch tabs or leave the page.
Is it hearing you? Make a noise

Your browser remembers that this site may use the microphone, so it won't ask each time. To be asked every time, open the site settings (the icon to the left of the address) and reset Microphone.

When your browser asks, choose Allow this time or Allow once (and leave Remember unticked in Firefox) to be asked again on every visit.
